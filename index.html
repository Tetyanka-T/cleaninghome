Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?

    No. Our team arrives fully prepared with professional cleaning supplies and modern equipment needed to complete the service. If you have specific products you prefer us to use, please let us know in advance.

  • Do I need to be home during the cleaning service?

    No. Many of our clients provide access instructions before the appointment. We respect your privacy and treat every home with care.

  • How is the cleaning price determined?

    Pricing depends on home size, bedrooms/bathrooms count, cleaning type, and property condition. We provide clear estimates before starting.

  • Do you offer recurring cleaning discounts?

    Yes. We offer weekly cleaning, every two weeks cleaning, monthly cleaning. Recurring clients receive priority scheduling and maintenance pricing.

  • How long does a cleaning appointment take?

    The time depends on the size and condition of your home and the type of cleaning selected. During your estimate, we can provide an approximate timeframe.
